About Voice of Truth

A Ministry Called to Reveal Truth, Restore Lives and Transform Communities

Our desire is to see people encounter GOD genuinely, walk in truth and become all that GOD has called them to be.

Prophet Jeremiah Rau, Founder & Overseer

Voice of Truth Incorporated began as a youth gathering in 2015 and has grown into a ministry committed to reaching individuals, families and communities with the Gospel of Jesus Christ. We believe that every person is valuable to GOD and that every life carries a God-given purpose.
