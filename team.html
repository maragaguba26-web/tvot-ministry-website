Our Leadership

Spiritual leadership

Servants called to lead and serve, guiding our ministry in its mission to align every soul to their God-given purpose.

Prophet Jeremiah Rau

Founder & Overseer

As Founder and Overseer of Voice of Truth Ministry, Prophet Jeremiah Rau provides spiritual leadership and direction, guiding the ministry in its mission to align every soul to their God-given purpose. Since its beginnings in November 2015, his leadership has helped shape the ministry’s growth and community outreach.

Pastor Maraga Jimmy

Senior Pastor

Working alongside the Founder and Overseer, Pastor Maraga Jimmy supports the spiritual care and guidance of the congregation, helping strengthen faith, encourage discipleship and nurture a church community committed to living out God’s truth.

Rehabilitation & Empowerment Centre

Project executive committee

Bringing experience in finance, management, public safety and community coordination to the planning and delivery of the Centre project.

Mr. Philip Isu

Project Chairman

Philip brings over 30 years of experience in Papua New Guinea’s financial sector, including investment and risk management. A former Head of Risk & Compliance at National Development Bank, he holds a Bachelor of Economics from UPNG and an MBA from RMIT, and is a graduate of the Australian Institute of Company Directors.

Mr. David Hase

Project Vice Chairman

A Chief Superintendent with the PNG Fire Service, David brings experience in fire safety, emergency response and operational leadership. He holds a Diploma in Human Resource Management and a Bachelor of Management from Divine Word University, supporting the project with leadership oversight and operational guidance.

Ms. Dorah Kaingen

Project Accountant

Dorah brings experience in accounting, financial management and donor-funded grants. She holds a Diploma in Accounting from ITI and a Bachelor in Business Management from Divine Word University. Her project role focuses on financial integrity, reporting and transparency.

Ms. Iamuia Irene Sale

Ministry & Project Administrator

Iamuia Irene holds a Bachelor of Arts in Population Studies & Social Work from UPNG and has experience in freight forwarding, shipping and logistics. She has coordinated fundraising drives, crusades and community programmes, and supports the ministry and project through administration, organisation and communication.
