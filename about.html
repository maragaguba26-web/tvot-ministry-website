Who We Are

Voice of Truth Incorporated

Voice of Truth Ministry (TVOT) is a Christian ministry committed to proclaiming the Gospel, nurturing authentic faith and serving communities through discipleship, outreach and practical care.

Our mission: To align every soul to their God-given purpose.

Our motto: “Every person is valuable to GOD.”

Our work is guided by the belief that every person has value and a God-given purpose. We seek to live this out through truth, faith, service and community relationships.

Our Story

Beginnings, Growth and a Place to Gather

The ministry’s journey has developed through gatherings, community relationships and a permanent church home in Baruni Village.

November 2015

Our Beginnings

Voice of Truth began as a youth gathering at Prophet Jeremiah Rau’s house at Air Transport Squadron (ATS), Port Moresby.

2019

Our Growth

The ministry moved to Baruni Village, Port Moresby, which remains its main centre.

2024–2025

Our Church Building

Following a fundraiser in September 2024, construction began in October. The building was completed in approximately three months, and the congregation moved in during January 2025.

Community Empowerment & Restoration
