We preach the Gospel, disciple believers, and help people grow in faith, truth and purpose through consistent teaching, prayer and pastoral care.

Youth & Community Outreach

Empowering young people to discover their God-given purpose and become positive influences in their communities. Through our Youth Empowerment Conference and youth outreach initiatives, Voice of Truth Ministry encourages spiritual growth, leadership development, and a commitment to building a stronger future for Papua New Guinea.

Community Empowerment & Restoration

Bringing community-minded leaders together to inspire unity, shared responsibility, and lasting change. Through our Motu-Koita Elites Seminar, Voice of Truth Incorporated encouraged Motu-Koita men and women to work together in building stronger communities, with a shared focus on the development of Baruni and Tatana.
